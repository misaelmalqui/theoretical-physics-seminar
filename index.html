Date: April 14, 2026 Session #01

Introduction to Loop Quantum Gravity & Area Spectra

Speaker: [Speaker Full Name]

We introduce the canonical quantization of general relativity using Ashtekar-Barbero variables. We construct the kinematic Hilbert space of spin networks and derive the discrete spectrum of the area operator $\hat{A} = 8\pi \gamma \ell_P^2 \sum \sqrt{j(j+1)}$.

References & Readings:
  • Rovelli, C. & Smolin, L. (1995). Discreteness of area and volume in quantum gravity, Nucl. Phys. B 442, 593.
  • Thiemann, T. (2007). Modern Canonical Quantum General Relativity, Cambridge University Press.

Date: May 02, 2026 Session #02

Entanglement Entropy & Holographic Dualities

Speaker: [Speaker Full Name]

A review of the Ryu-Takayanagi prescription for calculating von Neumann entanglement entropy $S_A$ in conformal field theories via minimal surfaces in asymptotic $\text{AdS}_{d+1}$ space.

References & Readings:
  • Ryu, S. & Takayanagi, T. (2006). Holographic Derivation of Entanglement Entropy from AdS/CFT, Phys. Rev. Lett. 96, 181602.
